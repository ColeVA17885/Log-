The 10 charts I check first when investigating a log. Anomalies detected by the analyzer are marked with red diamonds on every relevant chart — click an anomaly in the Anomalies tab to know when something happened.
1. RCIN — Pilot Stick Inputs (C1-C4)
Roll · Pitch · Throttle · Yaw. What the pilot was commanding.
2. Battery — Voltage & Current
Rapid V drops or current spikes = trouble.
3. Baro Altitude
High-rate altitude. Compare against GPS alt to spot baro issues.
4. |IMU Accel| — √(AccX² + AccY² + AccZ²)
~9.81 in cruise. Spikes near landing/disarm are normal. Mid-flight spikes = impact.
5. RCOU — Motor Outputs
Saturation (≥1900µs) or large spread = motor/ESC issue.
6. Attitude — Desired vs Actual (Roll / Pitch)
Gap between Des and actual = the controller can't follow demand.
7. EKF Normalized Innovations
Values > 1.0 = the EKF is rejecting that sensor.
8. GPS — HDOP & Sat Count
HDOP > 2 marginal, > 5 unsafe. Sats < 6 marginal.
9. Vibration X/Y/Z
> 30 m/s² is concerning. Clipping (Clip0) = severe.
10. Servo Rail Voltage (VServo)
Below 4.8V marginal, below 4.5V brownout risk.
